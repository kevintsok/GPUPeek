--- Key Findings ---
1. Memory-intensive kernels benefit from high occupancy (256-512 threads)
2. Compute-intensive kernels are less sensitive to threadgroup size
3. Shared memory usage limits effective occupancy benefit
4. Branch divergence reduces effective occupancy
5. Warp-level efficiency is independent of threadgroup size
