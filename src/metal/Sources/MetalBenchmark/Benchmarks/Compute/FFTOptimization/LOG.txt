--- Speedup (Shared vs Naive) ---
N=256: 1.19x
N=512: 1.08x
N=1024: 1.07x
N=2048: 1.10x
N=4096: 1.17x

--- Key Findings ---
1. Shared memory reduces global memory traffic
2. Radix-4 reduces number of stages
3. Performance scales with size
