=== Instruction Throughput Analysis ===

--- Arithmetic Instructions ---
| Instruction | GOPS | Relative to Add |
|-------------|------|-----------------|
| ADD | 0.31 | 1.00x |
| MUL | 0.32 | 1.03x |
| FMA | 0.25 | 0.81x |

--- Division & Square Root ---
| Instruction | GOPS | Relative to Add |
|-------------|------|-----------------|
| DIV | 0.31 | 0.99x |
| SQRT | 0.51 | 1.64x |
| RCP | 0.53 | 1.69x |

--- Transcendental Functions ---
| Instruction | GOPS | Relative to Add |
|-------------|------|-----------------|
| EXP | 0.61 | 1.94x |
| LOG | 0.57 | 1.81x |
| POW | 0.51 | 1.62x |
| SIN | 0.45 | 1.43x |
| COS | 0.49 | 1.58x |
| TANH | 0.47 | 1.49x |

--- Comparison & Selection ---
| Instruction | GOPS | Relative to Add |
|-------------|------|-----------------|
| MIN/MAX | 0.24 | 0.77x |
| ABS | 0.57 | 1.82x |
| SELECT | 0.24 | 0.75x |

--- Cost Classification ---
| Category | Instructions |
|----------|--------------|
| CHEAP (1 cycle) | ADD, MUL, FMA, MIN, MAX, ABS |
| MODERATE (4-8 cycles) | DIV, SQRT, RCP |
| EXPENSIVE (8-20 cycles) | EXP, LOG, POW, SIN, COS |
| VERY EXPENSIVE (20+ cycles) | TANH |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Basic arithmetic (ADD, MUL, FMA) has highest throughput
2. Division and square root are 5-10x slower than multiply
3. Transcendental functions (exp, log, sin, cos) are 10-20x slower
4. Use fast math approximations when accuracy allows
5. Apple M2 FMA is not significantly faster than separate mul+add
