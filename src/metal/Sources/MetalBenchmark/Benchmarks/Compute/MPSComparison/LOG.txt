=== MPS vs Custom Kernel Performance Comparison ===

--- GEMM (Matrix Multiply) Comparison ---
Custom Naive GEMM: 4.38 GFLOPS

Note: Apple MPS provides MPSMatrixMultiplication for optimized GEMM.
For Apple Silicon, use MPS for production GEMM operations.

--- Image Convolution (Sobel Edge Detection) ---
Custom Sobel (texture): 45.66 Mpixels/s
MPS Sobel: 13.68 Mpixels/s
MPS vs Custom: 0.30x (MPS is 3.3x SLOWER!)

--- Speedup Analysis ---
MPS vs Custom Sobel: 0.30x (MPS slower)

--- Key Findings ---
1. Custom Sobel with direct texture read: 45.66 Mpixels/s
2. MPS Sobel: 13.68 Mpixels/s (slower than custom)
3. Custom kernel outperformed MPS in this specific test
4. Possible reasons for MPS slowdown:
   - Different gradient magnitude calculation method
   - Additional edge handling in MPS implementation
   - Texture format mismatch (r32Float vs standard formats)
   - MPS overhead for simple operations

--- When Custom Kernels Win ---
1. Simple operations with known access patterns
2. When you need specific numerical behavior
3. When MPS overhead exceeds its benefits
4. For specialized or non-standard filters
5. Edge detection on Apple M2 GPU

--- When MPS Wins ---
1. Complex operations (CNN convolutions)
2. When hardware acceleration provides clear benefit
3. Production code requiring reliability
4. Larger-scale operations where overhead amortizes

--- Analysis Details ---
Custom Sobel:
- Direct texture2d read with explicit gradient computation
- Full control over filter coefficients
- Simple nested loop implementation
- 45.66 Mpixels/s on M2

MPS Sobel:
- Uses MPSImageSobel with hardware acceleration
- Standard gradient magnitude calculation
- May include additional processing
- 13.68 Mpixels/s on M2

--- Recommendation ---
1. Always benchmark your specific use case
2. Don't assume MPS is always faster
3. For edge detection, custom may be sufficient
4. For CNN convolutions, MPS likely faster
5. Consider texture format compatibility with MPS
