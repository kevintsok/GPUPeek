|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|

| 4096 | 18.935 | 6.362 | 5.937 | 6.355 | 6.321 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 7.032 | 6.103 | 5.790 | 5.959 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 8.732 | 6.834 | 6.551 | 6.451 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.230 | 7.733 | 8.384 | 7.400 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 55.980 | 14.721 | 15.296 | 10.453 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.85x | 1.68x | 1.73x | 1.68x |
| 4096 | 2.98x | 3.19x | 2.98x | 3.00x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
