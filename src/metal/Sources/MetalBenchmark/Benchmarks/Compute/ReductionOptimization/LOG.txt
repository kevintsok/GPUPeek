|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|

| 4096 | 19.446 | 6.393 | 5.991 | 5.615 | 5.657 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 6.987 | 6.316 | 5.862 | 5.783 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 8.630 | 6.702 | 6.233 | 6.370 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.709 | 8.196 | 8.538 | 7.889 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 57.268 | 12.096 | 12.009 | 9.596 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 2.19x | 1.70x | 1.76x | 1.74x |
| 4096 | 3.04x | 3.25x | 3.46x | 3.44x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
