| 4096 | 24.591 | 8.246 | 6.248 | 6.172 | 6.071 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 7.417 | 6.454 | 6.522 | 6.041 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.392 | 7.127 | 6.990 | 6.800 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.066 | 7.739 | 8.671 | 7.981 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 57.557 | 13.477 | 12.814 | 9.961 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.56x | 1.65x | 1.71x | 1.41x |
| 4096 | 2.98x | 3.94x | 3.98x | 4.05x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
