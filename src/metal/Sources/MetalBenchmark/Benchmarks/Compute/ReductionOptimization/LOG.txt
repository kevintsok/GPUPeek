=== Reduction Operations Optimization Analysis ===

|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|

| 4096 | 19.263 | 6.128 | 5.446 | 5.276 | 4.970 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 6.906 | 5.842 | 5.405 | 5.481 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.332 | 7.018 | 6.579 | 6.656 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 23.377 | 7.791 | 8.258 | 7.240 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 56.636 | 10.853 | 11.239 | 8.841 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 2.08x | 1.70x | 1.70x | 1.73x |
| 4096 | 3.14x | 3.54x | 3.65x | 3.88x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
