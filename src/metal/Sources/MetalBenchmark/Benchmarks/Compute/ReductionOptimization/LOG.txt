|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|
| 1024 | 9.544 | 5.460 | 6.511 | 6.004 | 6.118 |
| 4096 | 19.941 | 6.781 | 6.808 | 6.898 | 6.009 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 7.350 | 6.411 | 6.511 | 6.322 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.334 | 7.746 | 7.048 | 7.052 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.693 | 8.283 | 8.733 | 8.220 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 53.454 | 13.261 | 17.097 | 13.507 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.75x | 1.47x | 1.59x | 1.56x |
| 4096 | 2.94x | 2.93x | 2.89x | 3.32x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
