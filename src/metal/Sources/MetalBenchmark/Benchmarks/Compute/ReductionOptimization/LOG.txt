=== Reduction Operations Optimization Analysis ===

|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|
| 1024 | 8.615 | 5.925 | 6.137 | 5.599 | 5.982 |
| 4096 | 19.682 | 6.633 | 6.006 | 5.706 | 5.567 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 7.007 | 5.728 | 5.508 | 5.618 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 8.776 | 7.541 | 7.251 | 6.868 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.489 | 7.700 | 8.310 | 7.123 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 55.561 | 10.863 | 10.987 | 8.589 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.45x | 1.40x | 1.54x | 1.44x |
| 4096 | 2.97x | 3.28x | 3.45x | 3.54x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
