| 4096 | 19.533 | 6.830 | 6.088 | 6.286 | 6.103 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 7.639 | 6.308 | 6.441 | 6.503 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.341 | 7.376 | 7.061 | 7.027 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.689 | 8.276 | 9.065 | 7.919 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 57.393 | 14.413 | 16.303 | 12.871 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.65x | 1.73x | 1.71x | 1.71x |
| 4096 | 2.86x | 3.21x | 3.11x | 3.20x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
