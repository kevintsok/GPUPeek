| 4096 | 19.485 | 6.930 | 5.996 | 6.335 | 6.347 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 7.563 | 6.750 | 6.246 | 6.361 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.428 | 7.393 | 6.508 | 6.323 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.944 | 8.424 | 8.955 | 7.916 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 55.260 | 13.298 | 12.714 | 10.499 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 2.14x | 1.71x | 1.76x | 1.61x |
| 4096 | 2.81x | 3.25x | 3.08x | 3.07x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
