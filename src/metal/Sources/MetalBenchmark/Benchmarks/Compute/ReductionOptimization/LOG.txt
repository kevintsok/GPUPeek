--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.64x | 1.74x | 2.21x | 1.86x |
| 4096 | 2.91x | 3.28x | 3.47x | 3.58x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
