=== Reduction Operations Optimization Analysis ===

|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|
| 1024 | 8.995 | 5.045 | 5.454 | 5.446 | 5.215 |
| 4096 | 18.490 | 5.992 | 5.722 | 5.293 | 5.394 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 6.856 | 6.550 | 6.186 | 6.204 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.304 | 7.110 | 6.390 | 6.464 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 16.706 | 8.103 | 8.603 | 7.774 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 54.729 | 12.044 | 11.912 | 9.617 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.78x | 1.65x | 1.65x | 1.72x |
| 4096 | 3.09x | 3.23x | 3.49x | 3.43x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
