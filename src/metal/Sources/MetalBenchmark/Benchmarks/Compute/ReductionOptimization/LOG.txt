=== Reduction Operations Optimization Analysis ===

--- Sum Reduction Performance ---

|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|
| 1024 | 9.354 | 5.433 | 6.028 | 5.842 | 5.993 |
| 4096 | 19.049 | 6.743 | 5.946 | 5.707 | 5.225 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 7.545 | 6.406 | 6.116 | 6.102 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.983 | 7.673 | 7.266 | 7.332 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 17.723 | 8.025 | 8.795 | 7.930 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 56.419 | 12.326 | 13.894 | 13.078 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.72x | 1.55x | 1.60x | 1.56x |
| 4096 | 2.82x | 3.20x | 3.34x | 3.65x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
