=== Reduction Operations Optimization Analysis ===

--- Sum Reduction Performance ---
| Size | Naive (ms) | Parallel (ms) | Shared (ms) | Warp (ms) | MultiWarp (ms) |
|------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------|
| 1024 | 8.927 | 4.764 | 4.583 | 4.601 | 4.620 |
| 4096 | 17.468 | 5.759 | 5.052 | 4.978 | 4.875 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 6.467 | 5.368 | 5.041 | 4.853 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 9.075 | 6.547 | 6.040 | 5.868 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 23.162 | 7.480 | 7.940 | 6.870 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 52.814 | 11.400 | 11.411 | 9.010 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.87x | 1.95x | 1.94x | 1.93x |
| 4096 | 3.03x | 3.46x | 3.51x | 3.58x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
