| 4096 | 18.053 | 6.048 | 5.323 | 5.086 | 5.140 |
| 16384 | < 0.001 | 6.693 | 5.528 | 5.586 | 5.359 |
| 65536 | < 0.001 | 8.396 | 6.599 | 6.269 | 6.320 |
| 262144 | < 0.001 | 15.956 | 7.392 | 8.113 | 7.235 |
| 1048576 | < 0.001 | 54.484 | 10.774 | 10.774 | 8.449 |

--- Speedup vs Naive ---
| 1024 | 1.88x | 1.75x | 1.78x | 1.77x |
| 4096 | 2.98x | 3.39x | 3.55x | 3.51x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Tree-based parallel reduction scales O(log n) vs O(n) for naive
2. Shared memory reduction reduces global memory traffic
3. Warp-level reduction (SIMD) is fastest for small reductions
4. Multi-warp combines warp-level speed with threadgroup efficiency
