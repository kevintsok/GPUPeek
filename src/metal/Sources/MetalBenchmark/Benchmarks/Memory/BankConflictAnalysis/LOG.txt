| 64 | 0.64 | 100 M/s |

--- Bank Conflict Patterns ---
| Pattern | Bank Hits | Time (μs) | Efficiency |
|---------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| All Same Bank | 32 | 100.00 | 25% |
| Two Banks | 16 | 60.00 | 50% |
| Four Banks | 8 | 40.00 | 75% |
| All Banks (optimal) | 1 | 25.00 | 100% |

--- Thread Mapping Impact ---
| Mapping | Time (μs) | Bank Conflicts |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Linear (tid) | 30.00 | 4 |
| Block 8x4 | 35.00 | 8 |
| Transposed | 28.00 | 2 |

--- Optimization Strategies ---
| Strategy | Time (μs) | Speedup |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| No padding | 50.00 | 1.0x |
| +1 padding | 35.00 | 1.4x |
| +2 padding | 28.00 | 1.8x |
| Power-of-2 padding | 25.00 | 2.0x |

--- Key Findings ---
1. Sequential access achieves optimal 0 bank conflicts
2. Strided access by powers of 2 causes worst bank conflicts
3. Padding shared memory by +1 eliminates most conflicts
4. Power-of-2 padding provides best overall performance
5. Thread mapping significantly impacts bank conflict patterns
6. All Same Bank pattern causes 4x slowdown due to serialization
