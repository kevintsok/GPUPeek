=== Blit Engine and Async Copy Analysis ===

--- Buffer Copy Performance (BlitEngine) ---
64KB: 0.02 GB/s
256KB: 0.09 GB/s
1MB: 0.25 GB/s
4MB: 0.63 GB/s
16MB: 1.39 GB/s

--- Asynchronous Copy (Non-blocking) ---
64KB: 0.06 GB/s
256KB: 0.28 GB/s
1MB: 1.19 GB/s
4MB: 1.93 GB/s
16MB: 2.08 GB/s

--- Synchronous Copy (Blocking) ---
64KB: 0.00 GB/s
256KB: 0.04 GB/s
1MB: 0.14 GB/s
4MB: 0.30 GB/s
16MB: 0.69 GB/s

--- Fill Operation Performance ---
64KB: 0.01 GB/s
256KB: 0.03 GB/s
1MB: 0.13 GB/s
4MB: 0.38 GB/s
16MB: 1.17 GB/s

--- Batch Copy (4 operations per command buffer) ---
64KB: 0.04 GB/s
256KB: 0.14 GB/s
1MB: 0.44 GB/s
4MB: 0.88 GB/s
16MB: 0.90 GB/s

--- Key Findings ---
1. BlitEngine provides optimized GPU copy operations
2. Asynchronous copy allows CPU/GPU overlap
3. Fill operations are efficient for initialization
4. Batching multiple copies can improve throughput
5. Apple M2 unified memory affects copy performance
