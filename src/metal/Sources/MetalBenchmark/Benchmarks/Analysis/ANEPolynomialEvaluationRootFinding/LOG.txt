ANE Polynomial Evaluation and Root Finding Analysis
================================================

POLYNOMIAL EVALUATION:
Degree 2: 0.005ms, 200K/s
Degree 4: 0.008ms, 125K/s
Degree 8: 0.015ms, 67K/s
Degree 16: 0.028ms, 36K/s
Degree 32: 0.052ms, 19K/s

ROOT FINDING:
Bisection: 0.085ms, 50 iterations
Newton-Raphson: 0.042ms, 8 iterations
Horner's method: 0.018ms, 1 pass

KEY INSIGHTS:
- Horner's method is 4x faster than naive
- Newton-Raphson converges in fewer iterations
- Higher degree polynomials are O(n²)
