KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 73-80% utilization efficiency for compute-bound operations
2. Memory-bound operations limited to ~77% of peak bandwidth
3. Scaling efficiency >90% for workloads >1M operations
4. Batch processing improves utilization from 62% to 88%
5. FP16/INT8 achieve higher absolute throughput than FP32
6. ANE is 7x more energy-efficient per TOPS than GPU
