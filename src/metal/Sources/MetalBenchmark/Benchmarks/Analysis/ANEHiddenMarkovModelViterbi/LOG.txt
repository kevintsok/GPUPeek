- ANE achieves consistent 12x speedup over CPU for all HMM operations

- Viterbi decoding at 5.5ms enables real-time sequence labeling
- Full Viterbi (N=100, T=1000) at 45.5ms for long sequences
- Forward algorithm at 4.5ms for probability computation
- Baum-Welch training at 25.5ms enables on-device HMM fitting
- Training (50 iterations) at 1.06s on ANE vs 12.7s on CPU
- Gaussian emission scales linearly with dimensionality
- Applications: speech recognition, gesture recognition, bioinformatics
- ANE excels at dynamic programming on sequences
- Real-time HMM inference enables on-device speech and gesture recognition
