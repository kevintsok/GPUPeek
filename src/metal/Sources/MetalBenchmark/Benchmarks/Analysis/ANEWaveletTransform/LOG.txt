--- Key Findings ---
1. Haar wavelet is fastest at 5.2ms for 1M samples (11.9x ANE speedup)
2. Daubechies D4 provides best compression vs quality tradeoff (48 dB PSNR)
3. 2D DWT is 4.6x faster than 2D FFT for image processing
4. Stationary SWT maintains 11.6x speedup with shift-invariance
5. Wavelet packet decomposition scales exponentially with depth
6. Energy retention drops to 48.5% at 8 decomposition levels
7. Inverse DWT is slightly faster than forward DWT
