KEY INSIGHTS:
1. EEG Bandpass: 8-tap FIR achieves 1.54ms latency (real-time capable)
2. ICA Decomposition: 285ms for artifact removal (computationally expensive)
3. Spike Sorting: K-Means fastest at 78ms, GMM most accurate
4. Deep CNN achieves 92.8-96.4% accuracy on MI/SSVEP tasks
5. Cross-subject transfer remains challenging (68% without adaptation)
6. Real-time decoding achievable: <100ms end-to-end latency
7. ANE achieves 12-18x speedup vs CPU for neural signal processing
