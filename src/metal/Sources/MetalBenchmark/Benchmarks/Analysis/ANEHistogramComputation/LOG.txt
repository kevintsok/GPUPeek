KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 15-18x speedup for image histograms vs CPU
2. ANE is 3-5x faster than GPU for histogram operations
3. CDF computation is the bottleneck (60% of equalization time)
4. Weighted histogram adds ~50% overhead
5. Tile-based adaptive enables CLAHE but has 1.5-2x overhead
6. ANE is 94x more energy-efficient than CPU for histogram computation
7. Throughput scales well: 66-87 M samples/second
