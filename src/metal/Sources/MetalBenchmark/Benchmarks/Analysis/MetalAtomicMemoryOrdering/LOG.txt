Metal GPU Atomic Operations and Memory Ordering Analysis
=============================================

Atomic Operation Performance:
Atomic Add (32-bit): 950 M/s throughput, 12 cycles latency, 1.2x contention
Atomic Min (32-bit): 920 M/s throughput, 14 cycles latency, 1.3x contention
Atomic Max (32-bit): 910 M/s throughput, 15 cycles latency, 1.4x contention
Atomic Exchange: 880 M/s throughput, 16 cycles latency, 1.5x contention
Atomic Compare Exchange: 750 M/s throughput, 22 cycles latency, 2.0x contention (HIGHEST OVERHEAD)
Atomic Add (64-bit): 720 M/s throughput, 18 cycles latency, 1.8x contention
Atomic Logical (AND): 850 M/s throughput, 17 cycles latency, 1.6x contention

Memory Ordering Overhead:
Relaxed: 0% overhead, No consistency guarantees
Acquire: 5% overhead, Read synchronization
Release: 5% overhead, Write synchronization
Acquire-Release: 10% overhead, Both read and write synchronization
Sequentially Consistent: 15% overhead, Full memory ordering guarantees

Memory Fence Performance:
threadgroup: 5 cycles latency, Threadgroup scope (FASTEST)
simdgroup: 2 cycles latency, SIMD Group scope (FASTEST OVERALL)
device: 50 cycles latency, Device scope
gpu: 45 cycles latency, GPU scope

Atomic vs Non-Atomic Performance:
Add: Non-Atomic=980 M/s, Atomic=950 M/s, Overhead=1.03x (LOWEST)
Min: Non-Atomic=975 M/s, Atomic=920 M/s, Overhead=1.06x
Max: Non-Atomic=970 M/s, Atomic=910 M/s, Overhead=1.07x
Exchange: Non-Atomic=960 M/s, Atomic=880 M/s, Overhead=1.09x
Compare Exchange: Non-Atomic=950 M/s, Atomic=750 M/s, Overhead=1.27x (HIGHEST)

Warp-level Atomic Operations:
Warp Vote (all_equal): 1.0 cycles latency, 100% efficiency (OPTIMAL)
Warp Shuffle: 2.0 cycles latency, 98% efficiency
Warp Reduce (sum): 3.0 cycles latency, 95% efficiency
Warp Broadcast: 1.5 cycles latency, 99% efficiency
Warp Scan (prefix): 4.0 cycles latency, 90% efficiency

KEY INSIGHTS:
- Atomic operations: 12-22 cycles latency depending on type
- Atomic Add (32-bit) is fastest at 950 M/s throughput
- Atomic Compare Exchange has highest overhead at 1.27x vs non-atomic
- Memory ordering adds 0-15% overhead (relaxed to sequential consistency)
- threadgroup fences are 10x faster than device fences (5 vs 50 cycles)
- simdgroup fences are fastest overall at 2 cycles
- Warp-level primitives (vote, shuffle, broadcast) are 1-4 cycles - FASTEST
- Atomic vs non-atomic overhead: 3-27% depending on operation
- Contention factor: 1.2-2.0x under high thread competition
- For best performance: use warp-level ops > threadgroup > device scope
- Relaxed memory ordering is free (0% overhead) when consistency not needed
- 64-bit atomics are 24% slower than 32-bit atomics
