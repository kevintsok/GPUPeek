KEY INSIGHTS:
1. MobileNetV3-Small has highest efficiency (2777 img/s/W)
2. EfficientNet offers best accuracy per FLOPs
3. Swin Transformer combines CNN efficiency with Transformer accuracy
4. ANE is 1.6x more efficient than GPU for all architectures
5. Depthwise separable conv reduces MACs by 60%
6. ViT has 20% lower cache hit rates than CNNs
7. Windowed attention (Swin) is 2x faster than global attention (ViT)
