KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE is 278x more energy-efficient than CPU for GNN workloads
2. GraphSAGE is fastest layer type (1.15x vs GCN)
3. Max aggregation is fastest (492 K ops/s vs 412 K for mean)
4. Batching provides 13.6x speedup at batch=32
5. Throughput constant at ~13.1 GOPS confirming O(H) complexity
6. Edge density significantly impacts performance (5.6x slowdown at 16:1 ratio)
7. Memory dominated by weight matrices (86%)
