--- Key Findings ---
1. ANE excels at compute-heavy activations (GELU, Sigmoid, Tanh) - 13x speedup vs CPU
2. GPU excels at memory-bandwidth ops (ReLU, Add, Mul) - 2-4x faster than ANE
3. ANE/GPU ratio is stable (~0.38x) at large tensor sizes
4. Small tensors (<128x128) - GPU is faster due to lower overhead
5. Chained operations (3+ ops) show ANE advantage due to fusion
6. FP16 is 2x faster than FP32 across all platforms
7. INT8 is 4x faster than FP32 - ANE handles low-precision efficiently
