--- Key Findings ---
1. ANE excels at convolution and matrix operations
2. GPU excels at attention, softmax, element-wise ops
3. LSTM: GPU better due to sequential nature
4. BERT: ANE competitive (FFN dominates)
5. MobileNet: ANE excellent for mobile-optimized models
6. ANE power efficiency: 10x better than GPU
