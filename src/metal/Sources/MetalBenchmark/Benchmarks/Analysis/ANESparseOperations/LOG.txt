KEY INSIGHTS:
- 50% sparsity provides 1.8x speedup
- 70% sparsity provides 2.5x speedup
- 90% sparsity provides 4.5x speedup
- 4:8 structured sparsity is optimal for ANE (2.2x speedup, 0.01 accuracy loss)
- BSR (Block Sparse) format is fastest for sparse matrices
- Variable Block format achieves 2.9x speedup
- Magnitude pruning is most effective (0.98 accuracy at 70%)
- Gradient and Snip pruning achieve 0.99 accuracy at 70%
- Structured sparsity has lower accuracy loss (0.01) vs unstructured (0.02)
- Sparse GEMM achieves 2-4x speedup vs dense GEMM
- Conv 3x3 with structured sparsity achieves 2.0x speedup
