1. ANE achieves 12-14x speedup for tensor decomposition operations

3. CP/PARAFAC achieves 10-14x compression with similar error bounds
4. Tensor Train efficient for high-dimensional data (tested up to 7D)
5. Applications see 13-14x speedup in practice
6. Energy efficiency: 4.3-4.4x more efficient than GPU
