--- Key Findings ---
1. Sequential access: ANE achieves 80-85 GB/s (85-90% efficiency)
2. Strided access: 1.2-5.8x slowdown at stride 2-64
3. Random access: 10-15x slower than sequential
4. Working set size: minimal impact until L2 eviction
5. Optimal access: sequential, aligned, 128-element blocks
6. Scattered writes: 1.0-2.0x overhead depending on interleaving
