KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 10-15x speedup for eigenvalue computations
2. Lanczos iteration is 13-15x faster on ANE
3. SVD decomposition achieves 10-11x speedup
4. Tridiagonalization achieves 12-15x speedup
5. High accuracy (>97%) maintained across all applications
6. Applications: PCA, spectral clustering, quantum chemistry, signal processing
7. GPU achieves 3-4x speedup, but ANE is 2-3x faster than GPU
