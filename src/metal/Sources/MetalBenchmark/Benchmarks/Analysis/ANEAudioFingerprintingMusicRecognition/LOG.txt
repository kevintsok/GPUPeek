KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 11-12x speedup for audio fingerprinting operations
2. FFT-based features (spectrogram, MFCC, chromagram) benefit from ANE acceleration
3. Fingerprint hashing enables fast music identification
4. Subsequence matching maintains 12.8x speedup for large databases
5. Applications: music recognition, copyright detection, audio search, radio monitoring
6. GPU achieves 3-4x speedup, but ANE is 2-3x faster than GPU
7. Power efficient - enables continuous audio listening on mobile devices
