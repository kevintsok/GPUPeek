KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 12-14x speedup for matrix transpose operations
2. Channel permutation is memory-bound, limited by memory bandwidth
3. Strided access adds 0-300% overhead depending on stride
4. Gather operations are 40-50% faster than scatter
5. Out-of-place is 22-29% faster than in-place
6. Batch transpose achieves nearly 2x efficiency with batch=32
7. Applications: CNN im2col, data layout conversion, tensor reshaping
