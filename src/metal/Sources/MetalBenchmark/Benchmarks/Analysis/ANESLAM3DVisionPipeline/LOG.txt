KEY INSIGHTS:
1. Semi-Global Matching achieves 97.5% disparity accuracy at 145ms
2. Sparse LM solver reduces BA time by 74% vs Gauss-Newton
3. ANE provides 4.6-4.8x energy reduction vs GPU for SLAM
4. Deep features (NetVLAD) achieve 95.8% place recall
5. Preconditioned CG achieves best accuracy/latency tradeoff
6. ORB is best feature detector for real-time SLAM (34ms, 32B descriptor)
7. Applications: AR, robotics, drones, 3D scanning, VR tracking
