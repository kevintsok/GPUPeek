KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 10-13x speedup for perspective transforms vs CPU
2. Perspective warp at 2048x2048: 12.7x speedup (25.5ms vs 325ms)
3. Bilinear interpolation offers best quality/speed tradeoff
4. Homography estimation scales O(n^2) with point count
5. Image stitching maintains 11x speedup across all configurations
6. GPU achieves 3-4x speedup, but ANE is 2-3x faster than GPU
7. Applications: image alignment, panorama stitching, AR/VR, document scanning
