--- Thermal Throttling ---
| Temperature | Throttling | Performance |
|-------------|------------|-------------|
| < 35C | None | 100% |
| 35-40C | Light (10%) | 90% |
| 40-45C | Moderate (25%) | 75% |
| 45-50C | Heavy (50%) | 50% |
| > 50C | Severe (75%) | 25% |

--- Power State Transitions ---
| State | Entry (ms) | Exit (ms) | Energy (mJ) |
|-------|------------|-----------|-------------|
| Idle -> Active | 0.5 | - | 0.8 |
| Active -> Idle | - | 2.0 | 1.5 |
| Sleep -> Active | 5.0 | - | 8.0 |
| Active -> Sleep | - | 3.0 | 4.0 |

--- Key Findings ---
1. ANE is 5-8x more power efficient than GPU for neural workloads
2. INT8/INT4 offer best TOPS/W for quantization-friendly models
3. Element-wise ops have highest efficiency (12 TOPS/W)
4. ANE power scales sub-linearly with batch size
5. Thermal throttling rare on ANE due to efficient architecture
6. LSTM and Attention are power-hungry operations
