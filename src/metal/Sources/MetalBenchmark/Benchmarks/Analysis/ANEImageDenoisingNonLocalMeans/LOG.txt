KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 11-13x speedup for image denoising operations
2. Non-local means preserves edges while removing noise (PSNR 34.5 dB)
3. Total variation denoising is computationally intensive but effective (12-13x)
4. Bilateral filtering provides fast edge-preserving smoothing (12-13x)
5. BM3D-style block matching enables state-of-the-art denoising (13x)
6. Gaussian denoising fastest but lowest quality (11-12x)
7. Applications: photography, medical imaging, scientific imaging, video processing
