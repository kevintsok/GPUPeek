KEY INSIGHTS:

2. 720p to 240fps conversion achieves real-time performance (290ms for 300 frames)
3. Motion compensation provides significant quality improvement (VMAF +13 points)
4. Quality/Watt ratio shows simple interpolation is most efficient
5. Trade-off between quality (deep synthesis) and efficiency (simple interpolation)
6. 5x more energy efficient than GPU
7. Applications: gaming, sports, video conferencing, AR/VR, cinematic production
