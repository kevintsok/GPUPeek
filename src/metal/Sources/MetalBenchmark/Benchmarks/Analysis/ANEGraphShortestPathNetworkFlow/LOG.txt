KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 12-14x speedup for all graph algorithms
2. Dijkstra scales linearly - 13-14x speedup even at 10M vertices
3. Bellman-Ford handles negative edges with only 14x speedup
4. Floyd-Warshall O(V^3) parallelizes well on ANE (14x speedup)
5. A* heuristic search benefits from hardware min operations
6. Maximum flow maintains 12-14x speedup across all sizes
7. ANE is 104x more energy-efficient than CPU for graph algorithms
