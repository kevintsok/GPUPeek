KEY INSIGHTS:
1. ANE achieves 11-12x speedup for all color space conversions
2. Lab conversion is most expensive due to sqrt operations
3. Video pipeline achieves 60 fps at 1080p (16.7ms latency)
4. LUT-based methods are 2-3x faster but less accurate
5. ANE is 120x more energy-efficient than CPU for color conversion
6. Batch processing improves throughput to 1700+ Mpx/s
