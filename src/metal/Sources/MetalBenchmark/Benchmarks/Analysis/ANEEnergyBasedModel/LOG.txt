PHASE BREAKDOWN:
| Phase | Description | Time % |
|-------|-------------|--------|

| Gradient | dE/dx computation for sampling | 43% |
| Sampling | Langevin dynamics or Gibbs sampling | 25% |

| EBM-XLarge | 1852.0 | 170.52 | 10.9x |

KEY INSIGHTS:
- EBMs learn energy surfaces rather than direct probability
- Contrastive divergence for training achieves 10-11x speedup on ANE
- Langevin dynamics sampling benefits from parallel gradient computation
- Energy gradient computation (dE/dx) is the bottleneck (43% of time)
- Applications: image generation, classification, reinforcement learning, computer vision
