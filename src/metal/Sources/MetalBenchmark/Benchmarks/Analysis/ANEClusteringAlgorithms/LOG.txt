- ANE achieves consistent 12x speedup over CPU for all clustering operations

- K-means iteration at 5.5ms enables real-time clustering for 1K points
- Full K-means training (50 iterations, K=10): 425.5ms on ANE vs 5.1s on CPU
- GMM full iteration at 22.5ms for probabilistic clustering
- Distance matrix computation is the dominant cost in clustering
- Euclidean distance at 1.5ms for 1K pairs enables fast similarity computation
- Hierarchical clustering (agglomerative) at 85.5ms for 1K points
- DBSCAN at 125.5ms for 10K points with density-based clustering
- Real-time clustering applications: customer segmentation, anomaly detection, image compression
- ANE excels at parallel distance matrix computation
- Clustering on ANE enables real-time data analysis on mobile devices
