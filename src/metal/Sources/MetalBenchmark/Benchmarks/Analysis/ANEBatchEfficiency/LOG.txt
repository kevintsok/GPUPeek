--- Key Findings ---
1. ANE throughput scales linearly with batch size
2. ANE startup overhead (~0.5ms) dominates small batches (1-4)
3. ANE becomes throughput-advantageous at batch >= 8
4. ANE efficiency peaks at batch 32-128 (1.18x baseline)
5. CPU is best for minimum latency single items
6. GPU consistently 1.14x faster than CPU across all batches
