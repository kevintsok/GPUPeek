--- Key Findings from Built-in Benchmarks ---
1. BFS: fundamental graph traversal, used in pathfinding, social networks
2. PageRank: eigenvalue-based ranking, used in search engines
3. Graph algorithms often have irregular memory access patterns
4. Frontier-based approaches help manage parallelism
5. Applications: social networks, recommendation systems, road networks
