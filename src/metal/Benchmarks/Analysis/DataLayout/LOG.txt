=== SoA vs AoS Data Layout Analysis ===

--- Data Layout Performance Comparison (M/s = Million particles/sec) ---
| Count | AoS (interleaved) | SoA (sequential) | Hybrid |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|--------|
| 1024 | 0.3 M/s | 0.2 M/s | 0.2 M/s |
| 4096 | 0.8 M/s | 0.8 M/s | 0.8 M/s |
| 16384 | 2.9 M/s | 2.8 M/s | 2.9 M/s |

--- Analysis ---
Unexpected Result: AoS and SoA perform similarly on Apple M2!

Possible explanations:
1. Apple M2 unified memory may hide AoS strided access penalty
2. Cache line behavior differs from expected
3. Compiler optimizations may be compensating
4. Memory controller optimizes for both access patterns

--- Key Findings ---
1. Apple M2 shows minimal difference between AoS and SoA
2. Performance scales linearly with particle count (O(n))
3. All three layouts perform similarly
4. This suggests Apple M2 memory system handles both patterns efficiently
5. For Apple GPUs, data layout choice may be less critical than expected

--- Implications ---
- SoA still recommended for:
  * Better cache line utilization when accessing single field
  * Easier vectorization (float4 loads)
  * Better compression (when fields have similar values)
  * GPU memory access patterns that differ from CPU

- AoS may be acceptable on Apple M2 for:
  * Simpler code structure
  * When objects are processed holistically
  * When field access patterns are unpredictable

--- Performance per Size ---
1024 particles: ~0.2-0.3 M/s (baseline)
4096 particles: ~0.8 M/s (4x scaling)
16384 particles: ~2.8-2.9 M/s (3.5x scaling from 4K)

Note: Scaling is sublinear due to memory bandwidth saturation
