--- SIMD Operations Analysis ---
Apple Metal SIMD Operations (similar to NVIDIA Warp):

1. simd_broadcast
   - Copies value from one lane to all others
   - Lowest latency of all shuffle operations
   - Use for: constant propagation across warp

2. simd_shuffle
   - Exchange data between lanes with arbitrary pattern
   - XOR pattern optimal for power-of-2 distances
   - Use for: reductions, voting, data exchange

3. simd_shuffle_down/up
   - Shift data between adjacent lanes
   - Good for cascade operations
   - Use for: parallel prefix, scan

4. simd_any / simd_all
   - Vote operations across warp
   - Hardware-accelerated
   - Use for: branch voting, convergence checks

--- Reduction Algorithm Comparison ---
Sequential (within warp):
- 31 operations for 32 lanes
- 16.1% efficiency

XOR Shuffle:
- 5 operations for 32 lanes
- 100% efficiency (log2(32) = 5)

Key Insight: Always use shuffle-based reductions!

--- SIMD Group Size ---
- Width: 32 threads (fixed for Apple GPUs)
- Same as NVIDIA Warp concept

--- Optimization Strategies ---
1. Use shuffle instead of shared memory for warp-level exchange
2. XOR shuffle for power-of-2 reduction patterns
3. Broadcast for constant propagation
4. Avoid sequential within-warp operations

--- Apple GPU vs NVIDIA Comparison ---
